# TUAS guided payload

3D replay of a parafoil drop. Drag the view to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Follow sits behind the payload. Overhead and Fit reframe the drop. Sliders recompute in the page.

Calm air in this model lands inside the 50 ft ring. The inverted-PID preset is the 2025 sign bug and flies away. Pitch and roll on screen are display-only. These are model results, not a measured flight.
